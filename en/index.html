Welcome

Rest · Create · Be

Villa Amano is a small boutique hotel in Bilzen-Hoeselt, built around three simple ideas. The interior blends Japandi calm with a touch of Ibiza: earthy tones, warm light, linen, wood and stone. This page gives English-speaking guests the whole picture in one place; most detailed pages on the site are in Dutch, and we have marked them as such.

Rest

Sleep in one of five quiet gemstone rooms, book a private sauna session and let breakfast come to you. No lobby bustle, no crowds — just an access code and time for yourself.

Create

Our Pottery House is a real working studio. Sit at the wheel, glaze a bowl or join an evening workshop. You leave with something made by your own hands.

Be

Cycle through blossoming orchards, wander Roman Tongeren or simply sit on the terrace. Retreats and one-to-one sessions add space for reflection.

Create

Pottery House

Our ceramics studio is open until 22:00, for hotel guests and visitors alike. An open studio session lasts three hours and costs € 30 per person, including the wheel, clay, glaze and guidance. Sessions start at 10:00, 13:00, 16:00 and 19:00 — the late slot is a lovely way to end a day of sightseeing.

Fancy more? An 8-week course costs € 385, a 10-session studio card € 270 (valid one year) and unlimited studio access € 95 per month, cancellable monthly. A one-off membership of € 25 includes your Villa Amano apron. Pieces are fired and ready after about three weeks, to collect or have posted.

Rest

Private wellness

Sauna, steam room and a quiet relaxation area, open daily from 10:00 to 19:30. Sessions last two hours and start at 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00 or 17:30 (last start 17:30). The price is € 35 per person, with bathrobes, slippers and towels included. Hotel guests book their slot in the guest portal.

Coming back often? A 5-visit card costs € 160 (valid six months), or choose the monthly pass at € 110 for four visits a month with priority on time slots.

Getting here

Villa Amano is at Wijsstraat 1, 3740 Bilzen-Hoeselt, and welcomes guests by appointment or reservation only.

  • From Maastricht: about 20 km by car.
  • From Brussels: about 1 hour by car.
  • From Eindhoven: about 1 hour.
  • From Cologne: about 1 hour 30 minutes by car.
  • By train: Bilzen station is about 4 km away; continue by taxi or e-bike.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I book in English or French?
Yes. Our booking calendar and guest portal are currently in Dutch, but they are simple to follow, and you are always welcome to write to info@villa-amano.be in English or French. We reply in your language.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is between 15:00 and 21:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Late check-out until 13:00 costs € 45, subject to availability. There is no staffed 24-hour reception: you receive your access code on the day of arrival.
Is breakfast included?
Breakfast is € 25 per person per morning (the Ibiza Sunrise menu is € 28) and is served between 07:30 and 10:30, in your room or on the terrace. If breakfast is part of your booking, it is included per guest per day; you simply choose your menu in your guest account.
Can I cancel my booking?
Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before arrival, directly in the guest portal. After that, the first night is charged (for activities, the full activity price).
Is parking available?
Yes, parking is free for guests. Charging an electric car costs € 10 per day.
Do I need experience to use the Pottery House?
No. The open studio includes the wheel, clay, glaze and guidance, so beginners are very welcome. A one-off membership of € 25 (including a Villa Amano apron) is needed for open studio sessions and courses. Your fired pieces are ready after about three weeks and can be collected or posted to you.
Are pets or smoking allowed?
Villa Amano is entirely non-smoking and we cannot accommodate pets.
How do I get there without a car?
The nearest railway station is Bilzen, about 4 km away. From there you can take a taxi, and during your stay you can rent an e-bike from us for € 45 per day.
